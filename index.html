HOW TO PLAY

Step-by-Step Guide

  • 👀
    1. Watch (Dekhein): Grid ke kuch boxes Green hokar ek sequence mein chamkenge. Unhe dhyaan se dekhein.
  • 🧠
    2. Memorize (Yaad Rakhein): Un boxes ka order yaad rakhein.
  • 🖱️
    3. Repeat (Dohrayein): Jab upar message mein "YOUR TURN!" likha aaye, toh unhi boxes par usi same order mein click karein.
  • ❤️
    4. Survive (Bachein): Aapke paas 3 Lives (Dil) hain. Galat box click karne par 1 life kam hogi. (3 galtiyan = Game Over).
  • 🚀
    5. Level Up: Aapko total 10 levels paar karne hain. Har level ke saath grid bada hoga (3x3 se 5x5 tak).
